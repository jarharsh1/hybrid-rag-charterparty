# Voyage Charter Party (sample)

> Illustrative, fictional charter-party clauses for a Bahri-style maritime
> commercial use case. Not legal advice and not a real agreement.

## 1. Parties
This Charter Party is made between the Owners of the vessel and the Charterers
for the carriage of a bulk cargo.

## 2. Freight
Freight shall be paid by the Charterers to the Owners at USD 18.50 per metric
tonne of cargo loaded, payable within five banking days after completion of
loading and signing of bills of lading.

## 3. Laytime
Laytime for loading and discharging shall be 72 running hours, weather
permitting, Sundays and holidays excluded. Laytime commences when the vessel is
an arrived ship and notice of readiness has been tendered and accepted.

## 4. Demurrage
If the laytime allowed is exceeded, demurrage shall be payable by the Charterers
to the Owners at the rate of USD 25,000 per day, or pro rata for part of a day,
for all time lost waiting beyond the allowed laytime.

## 5. Despatch
Should the vessel complete loading and discharging in less than the allowed
laytime, the Owners shall pay despatch money to the Charterers at half the
demurrage rate for all laytime saved.

## 6. Notice of Readiness
Notice of readiness may be tendered at any time during ordinary office hours
once the vessel has arrived at the port and is in all respects ready to load or
discharge.

## 7. Cargo
The cargo shall be lawful merchandise. Dangerous goods may only be shipped with
the prior written consent of the Owners and in compliance with the IMDG Code.

## 8. Laydays and Cancelling
If the vessel is not ready to load on or before the cancelling date, the
Charterers have the option of cancelling this Charter Party.
